From team feedback to leading with a plan

Transform raw employee feedback into structured action with our Triage and Backlog workflows.

Triage: Separate Signal from Noise in Seconds

Processing daily feedback shouldn't feel like a second job. Triage gives you a focused, single-card view to clear incoming comments with speed and intent.

One Voice at a Time

Give every comment your full attention without the distraction of an endless feed.

5 Express Actions

Keep important topics, Dismiss simple praise, Reply to quick wins, Label for context, or Assign to another manager.

Seamless Delegation

Route topics to the right manager instantly so feedback reaches the correct department without delay.

Color-Coding

Categorize feedback using standard tags or create custom, color-coded labels on the fly.

Backlog: Your Strategic Workspace for Long-Term Impact

Where Triage handles speed, the Backlog powers resolution. Track ongoing topics, collaborate confidentially, and ensure zero feedback falls through the cracks.

Clear Accountability

Assign topics to specific managers so everyone knows who owns the resolution.

Centralized Action Hub

Keep all feedback requiring deeper reflection or team alignment in one organized workspace.

Private Manager Collaboration

Discuss sensitive topics, tag colleagues with @mentions, and align internally before posting a team response.

Multi-Action Resolution Hub

Close the loop your way: send a Kudos to celebrate a win, or launch a Poll to gather more data.
